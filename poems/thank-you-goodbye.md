"Thank You" is what I wish I could say to you.
Our hours taught me more than my weeks ever could.
You taught me how to love, and how to feel loved.
And so "Thank You" is what I say to my first true love.

Goodbye is the other half, the sadder half.
Even though we are not together, I wish you the best.
Go achieve all those dreams you told me about.
I am sure you will be able to, you have come so far.

Thank You, and Goodbye.
