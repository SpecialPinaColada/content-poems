---
title: Somebody That I Used To Know
date: 2026-10-13
tags: [love, breakup]
---
Somebody that I used to know,
I guess that's all you are now.
And yet your number stays in my phone.
And your face stays burned into my mind.
Notice how the world stands still for me,
While it continues revolving for everyone.

This morning I wake up empty,
not because I miss you anymore,
rather because I miss missing you.
And feeling this normal feels so weird.
And I realize I have finally moved on.
While I slowly rejoin the world, still living.
