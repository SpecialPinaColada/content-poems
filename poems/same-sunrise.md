Peace and pain.
What difference does it make now,
knowing I will never have you again,
while you will always have a piece of me?

That is the worst part of losing you:
I understand that you are gone, and yet
my heart hasn’t learned what it’s meant to do,
how to stop loving you.

For how could I say you were to blame?
You did nothing wrong, and neither did I.
And yet maybe we both did, just the same,
complicit in the demise.

We didn’t end from a lack of love,
and that is the cruelest part.
How do I move on from you
when I’d built a future we never got to start?

It hurts me that I will never know
your happily ever after,
whenever you may find it.
And find it you will. I believe in that.

And so I leave you with one last wish:
that if someday you think of me,
and if you see how deeply I loved you,
love me again in memory, where love can never fade. 

We, two people who once knew everything,
every hope, every fear, every secret,
will now slowly drift apart,
still living beneath the same sunrise.
