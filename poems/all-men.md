So you say not all men?
Tell me which ones to avoid
Tell them, the women, they deserve to know.

Is it the one who is openly misogynistic?
The one who flirts aggressively?
These are the easy ones to spot

But also the quiet one?
Waiting for his chance to strike
Without warning, something to be feared

The friendly one?
Remember, most domestic violence,
It comes from home

The one who says he’s different?
Why wouldn’t some of them lie?
After all they have nothing to lose

You see now,
You can’t tell!
And THAT is why the woman must guard herself

NOT ALL MEN intend to hurt her
But ALL MEN possess that power
And so it is ALL MEN

Now you get it.
And so I ask,
Pause and think, is it really

NOT ALL MEN?
